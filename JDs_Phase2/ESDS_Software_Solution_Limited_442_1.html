Junior AI/ML Engineer (Data Center Industry) Eligibility: Final Year / Recent Pass-out Students Degree: BE / BTech (Computer Science, IT, AI/ML, Data Science, ECE) Experience: Fresher Location: Work from Office (Data Center / Corporate Office) About the Role We are hiring fresh engineering graduates who are passionate about Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning and want to apply their knowledge to real-world infrastructure and data center problems. This role provides hands-on exposure to live systems, operational data, and industry use cases. What You Will Do Learn how AI/ML is used in data center operations. Work with real data such as server metrics, logs, and performance data. Assist in building ML models for: Predictive alerts Anomaly detection Capacity planning Process automation Support senior engineers in testing and deploying models. Create basic reports and dashboards. Document your work and learnings. Skills Required Basic knowledge of Python programming. Understanding of Machine Learning fundamentals. Familiarity with data handling concepts. Basic awareness of databases or SQL. Willingness to learn and grow in a technical environment. Good to Have (Optional) Academic project or internship in AI/ML or Data Science. Exposure to Python libraries like Pandas, NumPy, or Scikit-Learn. Basic knowledge of Linux. Interest in cloud computing or data centers. Who Should Apply Students who enjoy solving problems using data. Candidates with a strong learning mindset. Freshers looking for long-term technical growth. Those comfortable working from office and in structured environments. What We Offer Structured training and mentorship. Hands-on experience with real production systems. Exposure to enterprise-level infrastructure. Clear career path in AI/ML, MLOps, and Data Engineering. Opportunity to work with experienced industry professionals.
