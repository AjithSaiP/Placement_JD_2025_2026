Job Description

About the Role We are looking for passionate and curious graduates to join our Data Science team. As a fresher Data Scientist, you will work with experienced engineers and analysts to solve real business problems using data, statistics, and machine learning. This is an excellent opportunity to build a strong foundation in modern data science practices. Key Responsibilities Work with mentors to understand business problems and translate them into analytical tasks. Collect, clean, and prepare data for analysis and modeling. Build statistical models, machine learning algorithms, and exploratory analyses. Support dashboards, reporting, and data visualization efforts. Collaborate with cross-functional teams including Engineering, Product, and Business. Present findings clearly through reports, visualizations, and presentations. Continuously learn and adopt new tools, libraries, and best practices. Required Skills Strong understanding of statistics, probability, and mathematical concepts. Good knowledge of Python or R for data analysis. Familiarity with SQL and database concepts. Basic understanding of machine learning algorithms (supervised, unsupervised). Ability to work with large datasets and perform data cleaning. Good analytical thinking, problem-solving, and communication skills. Preferred (Good to Have) Exposure to data visualization tools (Power BI, Tableau, matplotlib, seaborn). Internship or academic project in data science, ML, or analytics. Knowledge of cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP) is a plus.
