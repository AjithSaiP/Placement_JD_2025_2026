Job Description

Fluxion operates in environments where mechanical failure costs crore of rupees per hour: port terminal equipment at Kandla, steel plant material handling systems, automated guided vehicle fleets, and EV manufacturing assembly lines. The mechanical engineer we are hiring will be working at the interface between these physical systems and the digital intelligence we are deploying on top of them. This is not a design office role. You will be on site. You will be assessing real plant, specifying real components, and working alongside electronics and systems engineers to make automated systems function in the physical world. If your ideal engineering career involves staying at a desk, this is not the right position.
