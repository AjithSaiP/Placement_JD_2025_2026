Structure Design Engineer Job Description Are you passionate about Structural Design Engineer? We are seeking a skilled Structural Design Engineer to join our team at SM Consultants PVT.LTD. RESPONSIBILITIES:  Working Closely In Partnership with Clients to Ensure Structural Design Goes As Per Plan.  Compliance of Designs According To Indian Standard Codes (IS & IRC Codes).  Responsible For Supporting Both Front End Design & Onsite Installation and Commissioning.  Responsible For Ensuring Projects Run Smoothly and Structures Are Completed Within Time, And Providing Economical Designs with Quality  Estimate and BOQ.  Coordination with different disciplines of engineering for various requirements in commercial, residential buildings and township.  Should be able to design all types of bridges (Road, Railway, Steel structures).
