Job Description

Mechanical Engineer – Motor Design Mechanical Designer – Electric Motors & Drivetrain Systems Role Summary We are hiring a Mechanical Designer to design, analyze, and validate mechanical components and assemblies for axial-flux motors, gearboxes, and motor-integrated systems. This is a hands-on R&D role involving CAD, FEA, and close interaction with motors, power electronics, and test teams. Key Responsibilities Design mechanical parts and assemblies for electric motors and drivetrains Develop detailed 3D CAD models and manufacturing drawings Perform tolerance stack-ups and GD&T for precision assemblies Support structural and thermal FEA for motor and drivetrain components Design housings, rotors, stators, shafts, brackets, and mounting interfaces Account for thermal expansion, vibration, and mechanical loads Support prototype builds, assembly, and design iteration Investigate failures and support root-cause analysis Work closely with motor, power electronics, and test teams Interface with suppliers and manufacturing for DFM feedback Required Skills & Experience Strong fundamentals in mechanical design and machine elements Proficiency in 3D CAD tools (NX / CATIA / Creo / SolidWorks) Experience creating manufacturing drawings and applying GD&T Basic to intermediate experience with FEA tools (ANSYS / Abaqus or equivalent) Understanding of bearings, shafts, fasteners, and material selection Familiarity with machining, casting, and fabrication processes Ability to work with tight tolerances and precision assemblies Hands-on mindset with prototype and hardware exposure Good-to-Have Experience with electric motors or rotating machinery Exposure to axial-flux or high-speed motor designs Knowledge of thermal management or cooling systems Experience with composite or lightweight materials This Role Is Not for You If You only want conceptual CAD without hardware follow-through You avoid tolerance analysis or design accountability You are uncomfortable working with prototypes or test setups What You’ll Gain Ownership of real hardware designs that go into production Exposure to high-performance motor and drivetrain systems Work in a fast, deep-R&D environment Clear growth path toward Senior Mechanical Engineer / Mechanical Architect
