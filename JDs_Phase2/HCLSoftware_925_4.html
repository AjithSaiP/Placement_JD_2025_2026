Job Description

The AI Research Analyst will support foundational and applied research initiatives in Artificial Intelligence, with a focus on emerging trends such as Large Language Models (LLMs), Agentic AI, multimodal systems, optimization techniques, and enterprise-scale AI adoption. This role is designed for high-performing engineering graduates with strong analytical and computational skills who can contribute to exploring cutting-edge AI concepts, benchmarking models, performing literature reviews, and developing prototypes that demonstrate the potential of new technologies. The analyst works with senior researchers, data scientists, and engineering teams to bring theoretical insights closer to practical implementation. Key Responsibilities • Conduct deep research on emerging AI methodologies, including LLM architectures, RAG techniques, agentic system design, reinforcement learning, and optimization. • Perform comparative evaluation and benchmarking of AI models, frameworks, and algorithms across performance, cost, scalability, and accuracy metrics. • Assist in creating technical research reports, whitepapers, proof-of-concepts, and internal knowledge artifacts. • Analyze scientific papers, patents, and open-source innovations to extract insights and identify opportunities for enterprise application. • Develop prototype implementations to validate theoretical concepts and accelerate experimentation. • Collaborate with AI engineering teams to translate research findings into practical solution components or platform capabilities. • Support innovation initiatives by evaluating tools, datasets, and workflows for internal use or customer offerings. • Stay updated with the rapidly evolving AI ecosystem—models, frameworks, safety practices, and responsible AI principles.
