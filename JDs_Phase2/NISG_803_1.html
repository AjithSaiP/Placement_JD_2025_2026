Job Description

Role Overview The Young Professional (YP) program at CRIS offers a unique opportunity for talented and motivated graduates from reputed institutions to work at the intersection of technology and governance. Selected candidates will collaborate closely with CRIS’s senior scientists, architects, and domain experts, contributing to the development of large-scale IT systems that serve millions across the country. Key Responsibilities Young Professionals are expected to: 1. Familiarize themselves with the working of Indian Railways’ IT ecosystem, understanding its challenges and opportunities. 2. Work under the mentorship of CRIS technology team leaders and architects, gaining hands-on exposure to real-world projects. 3. Receive guidance from internal technology teams and engage with external experts across diverse technological domains. 4. Identify problems, solutions, and innovations that create value for the Indian Railways IT ecosystem. 5. Develop and pitch project proposals, individually or in teams, to panels of technology champions and domain experts for review and feedback. 6. Collaborate with startups, industry partners, academic institutions, and professionals to explore joint innovations, in alignment with CRIS’s policies. 7. Rotate across business divisions and technology domains within CRIS to gain cross-functional exposure and identify areas of interest. 8. Demonstrate leadership qualities with the potential to lead technology teams in the future. 9. Pursue continuous learning and professional skill enhancement to drive innovation and excellence
