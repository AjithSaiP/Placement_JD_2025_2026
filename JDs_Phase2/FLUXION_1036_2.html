PLACEMENT Civil & Structural Engineer 1600000 INR

Job Description

The Port concession involves berth upgrades, quayside modifications for automated equipment, and foundation works for sensor and communications infrastructure across an active cargo terminal. Our manufacturing and warehouse automation deployments require structural assessments and modifications to accommodate new equipment loads and facility reconfigurations. The civil and structural engineer will be the technical foundation for all of this. You will work with port authorities, government engineers, and construction contractors. You will read and comment on drawings, coordinate site works, and represent Fluxion's technical position in meetings with clients and regulators. This is not a back-office infrastructure role. It involves direct engagement with government institutions and public-sector counterparts from early in your tenure.
