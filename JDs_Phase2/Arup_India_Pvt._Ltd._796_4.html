Job Description

Job brief/purpose To be a member of multi-disciplinary project teams to deliver high quality engineering services designs. Assist the project engineers and designers in performing detailed design and analysis for a range of civil and/ or building structures utilising numerical software tools. Work under the direct supervision of the Project Engineers and Designers. Coordinate with other internal/external project team members. Responsibilities • Working directly with our clients, architects, contractors and façade industry suppliers • Producing designs, assessments, calculations, drawings, reports and correspondence • Undertaking site, factory & testing visits and writing clear concise reports. • Undertaking failure investigation studies and provide clear reports. • Working with multi-disciplinary teams. • Working on several projects running in parallel. • Attending meetings and communicating with clients and design disciplines. • Receiving support from our senior team members. • Receiving training to develop your knowledge and skills. • Attending meetings and communicate with clients and design disciplines to establish their needs. • Prepare plans, designs, calculations, within the established codes, standards and other specifications. • Ensure timeliness and accuracy of own output by checking against required standards and agreed deadlines. • Implement assigned tasks in accordance with approved specifications and procedures. • Perform duties that may be assigned by the senior designers and engineers from time to time. Requirements We’re looking for graduates with integrity, enthusiasm and passion for engineering in the built environment. Qualifications & Skills • Recognised relevant qualifications in Engineering (preferred Civil or Mechanical) or Architecture Degree or similar area. • Excellent in preparing technical sketches. • Basic knowledge of the relevant software, designing in Revit/BIM/Rhino would be added advantage. • Proficient in the use of MS Office tools • Able to demonstrate innovative design ability, engineering skills and knowledge • Able to quickly develop successful team relationships • Able to work independently with minimal supervision • Ability to maintain personal effectiveness particularly during periods of higher than normal workload • Excellent communications skills (both written and verbal) • Proficiency in English language both spoken and written • Excellent planning, organisational and time management skills • Highly motivated and willing to take on new challenges • Flexible to travel for project/site work Behaviours • Flexible to the needs of the business, and changes to project plans • Demonstrate an organised approach to work and meeting deadlines • Demonstrate a creative approach to problem solving • Able to work effectively both individually and as part of a team • Able to use your own initiative, be inquisitive and proactive • Dedicated to the delivery of projects, and the development of the team and business as a whole We would be interested to hear of any community engagement/charity/voluntary work undertaken.
