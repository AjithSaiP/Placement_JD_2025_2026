PLACEMENT Applied Scientist — Pricing, Bidding & Decisioning 3300000 INR

Job Description

Overview Tolken is a seed-stage cross-border payments platform building ML systems that power real-time pricing, bidding, and risk decisioning. This role owns problems end to end — from model formulation to production — and works directly with Product and Engineering. What You'll Do • Design, build, and deploy ML models for pricing, bidding, and risk decisioning. • Formulate model objectives from first principles: loss functions, constraints, and success metrics. • Design and run experiments (A/B tests, offline evaluations) and iterate on results. • Monitor models in production, investigate regressions, and improve performance over time. • Build data pipelines and feature stores to support production-grade ML systems. What We're Looking For • Bachelor's or Master's in Computer Science, Machine Learning, Mathematics, Statistics, or equivalent. • 0–3 years of industry experience, or a strong new grad with research, internship, or project-based ML experience. • Strong Python skills: pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn, plus PyTorch or TensorFlow. • Solid ML fundamentals: supervised/unsupervised learning, model evaluation, regularization, feature engineering, statistics. • Experience (or coursework) in taking a model from formulation to working implementation. • Familiarity with SQL and data pipelines. • First-principles thinker who can formulate objectives, not just apply patterns. Nice to Have • Optimization, bandits, or decision-making under uncertainty (dynamic pricing, bid optimization). • Fintech, payments, or risk/fraud ML experience (academic or professional). • Model explainability tooling (SHAP, feature importance). • Cloud (AWS/GCP/Azure), Docker, or MLOps basics. • Research publications or competition experience (Kaggle, etc.).
