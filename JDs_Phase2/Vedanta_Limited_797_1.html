Job Description

To ensure adherence to quality standards across processes, products, and systems within the unit. The role involves implementing quality assurance practices, driving continuous improvement initiatives, and supporting operational excellence to meet organizational objectives. Key Responsibilities: 1. Quality Assurance & Control: o Monitor and maintain compliance with ISO standards and internal quality systems. o Conduct inspections, audits, and testing of raw materials, in-process, and finished products. 2. Process Improvement: o Identify gaps in processes and recommend corrective and preventive actions. o Support Lean Six Sigma and Kaizen initiatives for operational efficiency. 3. Data Analysis & Reporting: o Collect and analyze quality-related data to identify trends and areas for improvement. o Prepare and present reports on quality performance metrics to management. 4. Stakeholder Coordination: o Collaborate with production, maintenance, and safety teams to ensure integrated quality practices. o Liaise with vendors and suppliers for quality compliance of incoming materials. 5. Compliance & Documentation: o Maintain accurate records of quality checks, audits, and certifications. o Ensure adherence to statutory and regulatory requirements related to quality
