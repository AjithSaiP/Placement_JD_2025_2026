Job Description

Role: Data and Machine Learning Engineer (Power Market) Employment Type: Full-Time Role Category: Data and Analytics Experience: 0-2 years About us: GNA Energy is India’s Premier OTC Platform for the Power Market, dedicated to accelerating the adoption of green energy. We are a fast-growing and dynamic company committed to a sustainable future. Job Summary: GNA Energy is looking for a passionate engineer who can work across data engineering and machine learning domains to build analytical products for the Indian Power Sector. The role involves creating automated data pipelines, managing large-scale datasets, and developing ML models that solve real-world business problems. The ideal candidate will work closely with the Business Team and take end-to-end responsibility for the data and analytics process, including understanding, extracting, structuring, storing, and providing predictive insights. Responsibilities: Data Engineering • Build and maintain automated ETL pipelines using Python, Pandas, and AWS services. • Design and manage scalable databases (MySQL, Cassandra) and APIs (Django REST Framework). • Implement data ingestion, transformation, and integration workflows. • Optimize backend performance, ensuring data accuracy, security, and compliance. • Ensure data consistency, integrity, and security across all systems. Machine Learning • Design, develop, and deploy ML models for analytics and forecasting. • Experiment with ML algorithms (supervised, unsupervised, deep learning) and optimize performance. • Monitor and maintain production models, implementing updates when needed. Required Skills • Programming: Python (primary), familiarity with R a plus. • Data Engineering: Django, Django REST Framework, Pandas, MySQL, Cassandra, ETL workflows, web scraping. • Machine Learning: Scikit-learn, TensorFlow/PyTorch, Pandas, NumPy, Spark. • Cloud: AWS (S3, Lambda, RDS, Glue, Redshift, EC2). Qualifications: ● B.Tech/M.Tech in Computer Science, Mathematics, or related fields. Additional Skills: ● Demonstrated ability to work independently in a fast-paced environment and adapt to new technologies. ● Strong analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills ● Proactive and self-motivated approach ● Good communication and interpersonal skills ● Develop strong domain knowledge in the power sector. ● Detail-oriented with a focus on data accuracy and quality. ● Ability to work independently and as part of a collaborative team.
