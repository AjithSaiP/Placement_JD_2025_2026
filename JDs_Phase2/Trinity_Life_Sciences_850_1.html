Job Description

Highly skilled individuals actively collaborate with teams across offices to deliver robust strategic and analytical solutions tailored to client needs. The ‘Associate’ role offers a valuable learning opportunity in complex data analytics (both inferential and descriptive), synthesis, problem-solving, insight generation, and structured communication. In this role, you will work with our healthcare clients on a range of engagements, including opportunity assessments and forecasting, choice-based modeling to evaluate demand and identify drivers/barriers to product adoption, patient journey analysis, customer segmentation, market mapping, patient-level data synthesis and insight generation, performance tracking, marketing analytics, and sales force analytics. Key Responsibilities • Critically analyze problem statements and deconstruct them to align with business objectives • Gather and interpret data to generate insights using statistical techniques and custom modeling approaches • Articulate findings and key implications (“so-what”) through compelling, visually engaging reports • Actively participate in client and project team meetings to contribute insights and drive alignment • Ensure project compliance and uphold the highest standards of quality and rigor • Execute defined tasks independently with full ownership of deliverables and timelines • Effectively communicate results and recommendations to internal Trinity stakeholders and clients
