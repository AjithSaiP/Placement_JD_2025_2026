Job Description

Sileaf is actively seeking passionate and skilled Research and Development Engineers specializing in Electrical and Electronics Engineering to join our dynamic R&D department. In this role, selected candidates will play a pivotal part in developing various applications of BLDC motors and associated electronics, including motor drives, Electronic speed controllers, DC to DC converters, inverters, and remote monitoring/automation systems. The ideal candidates should possess a strong inclination toward innovation and a fervent passion for developing cutting-edge technologies that align with our commitment to renewable energy solutions. They must also demonstrate strong analytical and problem-solving abilities, creativity, effective communication, and teamwork skills, along with proficiency in relevant software and design tools. Our R&D department offers an enriching environment where engineers actively participate in the entire product development lifecycle. From conceptualization to design, prototyping, testing, and final product realization, engineers are deeply involved in creating innovative solutions. This hands-on experience provides a comprehensive understanding of product development processes and the opportunity to contribute significantly to advancements in BLDC motor technologies and renewable energy applications.
