About Mphasis: Mphasis X2C2TM formula for success (Powered by Cloud and Cognitive), drives multi-dimensions of business value with an integrated consumer centric Front2Back™ Digital Transformation, enabling Business Operations and Technology Transformation. For more details, please login our website www.mphasis.com. Job Profile: Gen AI Software Engineer We are seeking a highly skilled GenAI Developer to join our dynamic, global team. The ideal candidate will have a strong background in applied generative AI. This role will involve developing and implementing AI solutions, working with various technologies, and collaborating with cross functional teams to drive innovation. The GenAI Developer will play a crucial role in advancing our GenAI capabilities. Key Responsibilities: • AI Integration: Develop and integrate AI-powered features, chatbots, and AI agents into business processes using frameworks like Spring AI, LangChain, or LangChain4j. • Backend Development: Design, develop, and maintain scalable, secure backend services and RESTful APIs using Java and Spring Boot. • RAG Architecture: Implement Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) models, working with vector databases (e.g., Pinecone, Qdrant, pgvector) to provide models with enterprise specific data. • Prompt Engineering & LLMs: Integrate enterprise-grade LLMs (like GPT, Claude, Gemini) and develop effective prompt engineering strategies. • Scalability & DevOps: Ensure the scalability, security, and performance of applications, often using cloud platforms (AWS, Azure) and containerization technologies like Docker and Kubernetes. • Collaboration: Work closely with data scientists, front-end developers, and product teams to ensure cohesive, goal-oriented solutions. Target Audience: Candidates currently pursuing BE/B.Tech graduates (2026 Pass out batch) from Computer Science, Information Technology, Electronics and Communication and other Computational sciences branches. Skills Required Successful candidates typically possess a strong blend of traditional Java development expertise and emerging AI skills: • Expertise in Java: Strong professional experience with the Java programming language and the Spring Framework, especially Spring Boot 3.x. • AI Frameworks: Hands-on experience with Spring AI or exposure to Python-based frameworks like LangChain for building LLM applications. • Cloud & Databases: Proficiency with cloud services (AWS, Azure) and various databases (PostgreSQL, MongoDB, vector databases). • Software Best Practices: Strong understanding of microservices architecture, clean code practices, testing (unit and integration tests), and CI/CD pipelines. • Problem-Solving: Strong in Data Structures and Algorithms. Desirable Qualifications (not mandatory) 1. Experience with AI/MLOps 2. Pre-training of self-supervised learning & representation learning 3. Fine-tuning AI/LLM models 4. Experience in model optimization and edge deployment Work Location: Bangalore-Hyderabad (Work from office) CTC – 11LPA Assessment/Selection Criteria: 1. Profile Screening 2. Coding/Technical Round – Data Structures & Algorithms Coding Test followed with Technical Discussion. 3. HR Discussion
