Job Description

We are seeking a motivated and enthusiastic Full Stack Developer Intern to join our innovative technology team. This role is ideal for recent graduates or early-career professionals with foundational skills in both frontend and backend development, particularly with Angular, Node.js, and TypeScript. During the internship, you will gain hands-on experience across the full software development lifecycle, with the possibility of a full-time role upon successful completion. Job Description_ Full Stack Dev… Internship Location: India (Remote) Job Work Location (post-internship): India or Netherlands Internship Duration: 6 months Stipend: ₹35,000 per month Key Responsibilities Develop and enhance user interface components using Angular, TypeScript, and potentially Ionic framework. Build and maintain backend services using Node.js and TypeScript. Implement robust REST API integrations, WebSocket communications, and authentication/authorization features (JWT and other security protocols). Ensure technical feasibility of UI/UX designs and translate them into responsive, visually appealing interfaces. Integrate multiple data sources and databases into unified systems. Maintain and improve application security, performance, and scalability across devices. Collaborate closely with cross-functional teams (frontend, backend, and design) to deliver cohesive solutions. Participate in code reviews, maintain code integrity using Git, and document processes for compliance and future reference. Optimize applications for speed, responsiveness, and cross-browser compatibility. Required Qualifications Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field. Proven experience with Angular, Node.js, and TypeScript. Familiarity with web markup (HTML5, CSS3) and server-side CSS pre-processors (LESS/SASS). Strong understanding of authentication/security protocols such as JWT. Knowledge of client-side scripting, asynchronous request handling, AJAX, and cross-browser compatibility. Proficiency with Git and version control best practices. Good problem-solving skills and ability to think algorithmically. Ability to perform in a fast-paced, team-oriented environment. Preferred Skills Practical experience with the Ionic framework. Familiarity with front-end build pipelines and tools. Exposure to serverless architectures and AWS cloud services. Experience with Python is a plus. Strong analytical skills and attention to detail. What We Offer A 6-month paid internship with a stipend of ₹35,000 per month. Internship will be conducted remotely from India. Opportunity to work on full-stack projects with a team of experienced developers. A supportive learning environment with mentorship and career growth opportunities. Based on performance during the internship, a full-time opportunity may be offered, with work location in India or the Netherlands.
