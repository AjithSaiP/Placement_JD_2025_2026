Job Description

About the Role Partner with product, growth, and founders to answer business-critical questions, translate requests into well-scoped analyses, spot trends and patterns in data, apply statistical thinking, build dashboards and reports, define core metrics, run deep-dive analyses, own A/B testing, build forecasts, and take full ownership of insights end-to-end. What You'll Do Partner with product, growth, and founders to answer the questions that actually move the business Translate vague requests into sharp, well-scoped analyses — and push back when the question is the wrong one Spot trends, patterns, and anomalies in product, user, and revenue data — and explain what’s actually driving them, not just what correlates Apply statistical thinking — distributions, variance, seasonality, base rates, regression, segmentation — to turn noisy data into reliable insights Build dashboards, reports, and self-serve views that people actually use and trust Define and document core metrics in a semantic / metrics layer (dbt Semantic Layer / MetricFlow, Cube, LookML) — what they mean, how they’re computed, when to (and not to) use them Run deep-dive analyses on funnels, retention, cohorts, experiments, and revenue Own A/B test design, sizing, and read-outs — including significance, power, MDE, CUPED / variance reduction, and the difference between “no effect” and “underpowered” Build forecasts and early-warning signals for key metrics so the team sees changes coming, not after the fact Take full ownership of insights end-to-end — from question, to data, to recommendation, to follow-up Proactively identify edge cases — bad data, survivorship bias, Simpson’s paradox, leaky funnels, timezone bugs, double counting — before they reach an exec deck Use AI-augmented analytics — text-to-SQL, AI copilots, narrative generation — to move faster without skipping rigor Work closely with data engineering on data contracts and source-system fixes rather than papering over quality issues in SQL What We're Looking For Strong product sense — can sit with the product team, understand the why behind a request, and reframe the question when needed Ownership mindset — treats every analysis as yours; follows it through to a decision, not just a chart Statistical intuition — comfortable with distributions, variance, correlation vs. causation, base rates, and confidence intervals; knows when a pattern is real and when it’s noise Pattern recognition — can look at messy data and quickly spot trends, seasonality, segments, and outliers worth digging into Insight generation — goes beyond reporting numbers; explains the why, recommends an action, and quantifies the expected impact Edge-case thinking — instinctively asks “what would make this wrong?” — selection bias, survivorship bias, Simpson’s paradox, missing data, timezone bugs, double counting — before publishing Storytelling — turns numbers into a clear narrative with a recommendation, not a wall of charts Fluent in vibe coding — comfortable using AI tools (Claude Code, Cursor, Copilot, ChatGPT) to write SQL, scripts, and analyses faster without losing rigor Expert-level SQL — window functions, CTEs, query optimization, comfortable querying messy production data Strong Python fundamentals for analysis (pandas, numpy, scipy / statsmodels, scikit-learn for basic modeling, plotting) Hands-on with a BI tool — Looker, Metabase, Mode, Tableau, Superset, or similar Solid grasp of statistics — hypothesis testing, regression, significance, power, sample sizing, time-series basics, and the common pitfalls behind each Experience with product analytics tools — Amplitude, Mixpanel, PostHog, GA4 Working knowledge of dbt and the modern data stack — enough to read models, debug lineage, and propose changes upstream Clear written communication; works well in async, low-meeting environments Nice to Have Causal inference — diff-in-diff, propensity scoring, instrumental variables, synthetic control, or quasi-experiments when A/B isn’t possible Time-series analysis and forecasting — ARIMA, Prophet, seasonal decomposition, anomaly detection Bayesian thinking — priors, posteriors, and why they matter when data is thin Experimentation platforms (Statsig, GrowthBook, Eppo) and switchback / interleaved designs Warehouse experience — Snowflake, BigQuery, Redshift, Databricks; warehouse-native ML / AI (Cortex, BigQuery ML) Familiarity with LLM / AI APIs for accelerating analysis and building data-aware tools, copilots, or text-to-SQL workflows Exposure to lakehouse formats — Iceberg, Delta, Hudi — and reading from open table formats in analyses Prior startup or 0→1 experience — comfortable being the first analyst Benefits & Perks - Hybrid work culture - Full time employment - Medical Insurance (Self + Family) - Life Insurance
