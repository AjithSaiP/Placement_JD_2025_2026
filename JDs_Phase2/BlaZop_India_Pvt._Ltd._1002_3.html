Role Overview We’re hiring Cloud Engineers with strong expertise in cloud infrastructure (AWS, Azure, GCP), Infrastructure-as-Code (Terraform, Ansible, Kubernetes), and DevOps tooling. This is a high-impact role where you’ll help build and manage our cloud-native SaaS infrastructure, deliver scalable microservices, and support platform evolution. Responsibilities • Design and implement scalable cloud solutions across AWS, Azure, and GCP. • Deploy, manage, and optimize containerized microservices. • Maintain CI/CD pipelines and automate infrastructure using Terraform and Ansible. • Troubleshoot cloud deployments and ensure high availability and performance. • Collaborate with development and DevOps teams to improve infrastructure strategies. Skills Required • Strong Python programming and scripting experience. • Expertise in AWS, Azure, or GCP. • Hands-on experience with Terraform, Kubernetes, and Ansible. • Solid understanding of Linux administration and infrastructure security. • Familiarity with database operations and monitoring tools
