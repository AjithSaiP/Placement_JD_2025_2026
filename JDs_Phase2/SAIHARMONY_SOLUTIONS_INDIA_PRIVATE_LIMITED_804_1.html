Job Description

As a Software Engineer (SWE) at SAIGroup, you will play a critical role in designing, building, and managing the services and components of an innovative enterprise AI platform to deliver transformative, AI-enabled work experiences for enterprise users. What You'll Do  Design, develop and operate services and components for our enterprise AI platform, using a cloud-first, cloud-agnostic approach with world class AI capabilities, scalability, security, privacy and operability.  Collaborate closely with a cross-functional team of AI researchers/engineers, software engineers, architects, product managers, and designers to translate complex needs into efficient, modular, and scalable platform components.  Ensure seamless integration of infrastructure, software, data systems, AI and UX components into the platform, guaranteeing smooth operation and dependable outcomes and experiences for our customers and partners. Requirements  Proficiency in containerization and orchestration frameworks like Docker and Kubernetes is essential.  Experience with common infrastructure-as-code frameworks for deploying and managing cloud environments.  In-depth experience across the full-stack development lifecycle, including design, prototyping, building, testing, deployment, and operations of enterprise platforms and products.
