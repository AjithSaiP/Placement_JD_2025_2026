Job Description

POSITION OVERVIEW Esyasoft Holding is a Dubai-based energy transition technology company which has its operations in India, UAE and other global locations. It has recently achieved unicorn status, marking its position as a leading innovator in the sustainable energy sector. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA • Bachelor's degree (B.E./B.Tech) in Computer Science, Mechanical, Electrical, Electronics, or Instrumentation Engineering • 2026 graduates (fresher candidates only) • Strong academic record with minimum 85% in 12th or 7.5 in CGPA • No active backlogs at the time of joining • Passion for software development and problem-solving KEY RESPONSIBILITIES • Develop, test, and maintain software applications for energy sector solutions • Collaborate with cross-functional teams to design and implement technical solutions • Write clean, efficient, and well-documented code following industry best practices • Participate in code reviews and contribute to continuous improvement initiatives • Debug and troubleshoot software issues in development and production environments • Work on integration of hardware systems with software applications (for Electrical/Electronics/Instrumentation graduates) • Support data analysis and visualization for energy management systems • Learn and adapt to new technologies and frameworks as per project requirements REQUIRED SKILLS & COMPETENCIES Technical Skills: • Programming knowledge in at least one language: Python, Java, C++, or JavaScript • Understanding of data structures, algorithms, and object-oriented programming • Basic knowledge of databases (SQL/NoSQL) • Familiarity with version control systems (Git preferred) • Understanding of software development lifecycle (SDLC) Domain-Specific Advantages: • Computer Science: Web development, mobile apps, cloud computing, AI/ML basics • Mechanical: CAD/CAM software, simulation tools, industrial automation • Electrical/Electronics: Embedded systems, IoT, microcontrollers, circuit design • Instrumentation: Sensor integration, SCADA systems, process control, data acquisition Soft Skills: • Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities • Excellent communication skills (written and verbal) • Team player with ability to work in collaborative environments • Quick learner with eagerness to acquire new technical skills • Attention to detail and commitment to quality WHAT WE OFFER • Comprehensive training program with hands-on project experience • Mentorship from experienced software engineers and domain experts • Exposure to international projects and cutting-edge energy technologies • Competitive compensation package • Opportunities for career growth and skill development • Work locations: Mangalore (India) WORK LOCATION India: PAN India HOW TO APPLY Interested candidates should submit their resume, SELECTION PROCESS Candidates with a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, Electronics Engineering, Instrumentation Engineering, or Mechanical Engineering , Hydraulic Engineering are eligible. Experience working on AI projects will be considered an added advantage. 1. Resume screening 2. Online assessment (technical + aptitude) 3. Technical interview + HR Interview 4. Final offer DURATION & EMPLOYMENT TYPE: • Full-time permanent position • Training period: 3-6 months • Long-term career growth opportunities ________________________________________ Note: Only shortlisted candidates will be contacted for further rounds.
