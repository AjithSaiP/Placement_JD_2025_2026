Role Overview We’re looking for someone who is smart, analytical, and has strong business acumen, someone who thrives in fast-paced environments and is eager to take ownership from day one. You don’t need years of experience; what matters most is your ability to think critically, solve problems creatively, and communicate effectively. If you're a quick learner with a passion for education, technology, and impact-driven work, this role will give you the perfect launchpad to grow into a top-tier product or strategy professional. Have a real impact on your work ● Top-of-the-line compensation. Want to be part of something meaningful and transformative? We’d love to hear from you!!
