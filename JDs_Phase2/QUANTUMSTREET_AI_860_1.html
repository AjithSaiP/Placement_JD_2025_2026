Job Description

QuantumStreet AI is an IBM backed fintech based in San Francisco and Bengaluru. It is a global leader in the application of AI/ ML to investment strategies and counts some of the most prestigious banks, pension funds, and wealth managers among its clients. Role Description We are looking for motivated candidates to join us as Associate Quantitative Analyst in the Investment Science Lab in Bengaluru. Selected candidates will work at the intersection of finance and data science, and contribute to projects enabling the management of multiple billions USD in assets. Candidates will collaborate with cross-functional teams, leveraging cutting-edge tools to improve portfolio performance and risk assessment that help deliver superior outcomes for our clients. Key Skills and Responsibilities Candidates need to have excellent skills in Statistics, Data Science, and Investment Analysis. General awareness of financial markets and the global economy would be an added advantage. Command of programming languages (esp Python), SQL, and model deployment pipelines is expected. 1. Data Analysis & ML Modelling: Analyse structured and unstructured financial datasets to extract meaningful insights. Build and implement predictive models, statistical analyses, and machine learning solutions for investment optimization and wealth management strategies. Work on time series forecasting, unstructured data analysis, knowledge graphs, risk modelling, and portfolio performance analytics. 2. Data Engineering & Integration: Work with large financial datasets, ensuring data quality, cleansing, and integration from multiple sources (internal systems, market data providers, etc.) 3. Research & Development: Conduct exploratory data analysis (EDA) and hypothesis testing. Develop and evaluate new models for forecast and optimization. 4. Portfolio creation and performance analysis: Create optimised portfolios for specific client needs. Analyse performance of portfolios and create material for client communication.
