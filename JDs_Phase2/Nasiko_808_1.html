Job Description

As a Backend Engineer at Nasiko, you’ll be responsible for building and maintaining high-performance backend systems using Golang/Python, while collaborating closely with ML and platform engineers to deploy AI-powered services and intelligent features. You’ll design distributed infrastructure, optimize real-time inference pipelines, and create APIs that serve models and data at scale. Your work will directly support the development of AI-driven products and internal tooling across Nasiko’s core platform. Skills and Qualifications Strong experience with Golang/Python. Solid understanding of distributed systems, concurrency, and network programming. Familiarity with machine learning frameworks such as PyTorch, TensorFlow, or JAX. Experience with model serving infrastructure (e.g., Triton Inference Server, TorchServe, ONNX Runtime, Ray Serve). Proficiency with containerization (Docker) and orchestration (Kubernetes). Knowledge of MLOps tools (e.g., MLflow, Weights & Biases, Kubeflow, or Airflow). Experience with cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, or Azure) and infrastructure-as-code. Comfortable with databases (SQL/NoSQL), caching systems (Redis), and message queues (Kafka, RabbitMQ). Strong Git and GitHub/GitLab workflows. Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or related field is a plus.
