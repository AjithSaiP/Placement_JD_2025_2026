Job Description

Fluxion's data science function is operational, not academic. The models we build run in production environments where their outputs influence decisions worth crore of rupees: sinter plant coke dosing, blast furnace thermal management, port berth utilisation, and clinical patient flow. A model that achieves high accuracy on a held-out dataset but drifts silently in production, or produces outputs no operator can interpret, is not useful. We are looking for someone from an engineering, mathematics, or computer science background who builds analytical systems with the same rigour they would apply to a physical design. You will work on real plant data from deployment day one. The problems are not textbook problems: the data is noisy, the labels are delayed or absent, and the failure consequences are visible immediately.
