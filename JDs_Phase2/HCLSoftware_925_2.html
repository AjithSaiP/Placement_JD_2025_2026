Job Description

The Agentic AI Engineer II will contribute to the design, development, and optimization of agentic AI systems, multi-agent orchestration frameworks, and advanced Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) workflows. The role focuses on building intelligent, autonomous agents capable of reasoning, planning, and interacting across enterprise systems while ensuring robust performance, accuracy, and scalability. As high-potential engineers from premier institutions, they will work closely with senior architects and AI teams to deliver next-generation AI capabilities for real-world enterprise applications.Key Responsibilities :- 1. Develop and enhance agentic workflows using LLM-based agents, decision-making loops, and orchestration frameworks. 2. Implement and optimize RAG pipelines including document ingestion, chunking strategies, embedding generation, vector search, and retrieval ranking. 3. Build and maintain reusable components for agent orchestration frameworks (state management, tool invocation, memory modules, guardrails). 4. Assist in constructing multi-agent systems that collaborate, negotiate, and coordinate tasks for complex enterprise workflows. 5. Work with senior engineers to integrate agents with APIs, microservices, and enterprise platforms for end-to-end automation. 1 6. Contribute to experimentation, evaluation, and benchmarking of agent behavior, retrieval quality, and task success rates. 7. Participate in full lifecycle engineering: design discussions, code reviews, testing, and documentation. 8. Troubleshoot issues, analyze logs and outputs, and propose improvements to reliability and performance. 9. Continuously learn emerging AI techniques, frameworks, and best practices to elevate solution maturity.
