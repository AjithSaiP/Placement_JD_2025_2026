Job Description

1. Subsurface Exploration and Sampling: a. Geophysical investigation:- 1. Electrical resistivity test (ERT) 2. Seismic refraction test (SRT) 3. Cross hole seismic test (CHST) and down hole seismic test (DHST) 4. Spectral analysis of surface wave (SASW) and multi-channel analysis of surface wave (MASW) 5. Ground penetrating radar survey (GPR Survey) 6. Thermal resistivity test b. Trial Pits c. Subsurface soundings 1. Dynamic cone penetration test (DCPT) 2. Static cone penetration test (SCPT) 3. Static cone penetration test using piezocone with pore water pressure measurement (CPTu) d. Boring in soil 1. Auger boring, 2. Shell and auger boring, 3. Rotary boring, and 4. Percussion drilling. i. SPT ii. UDS e. Core drilling in rock 1. Core recovery 2. RQD 3. RMR 4. Type of rocks as per the weathering characteristic f. Location and depth of investigation 1.2 In-situ/Field Testing a) Standard penetration test (SPT) b) Vane shear test (VST) c) Pressuremeter test (PMT) d) Plate load test (PLT) e) Cyclic plate load test (CPLT) f) Block vibration test (BVT) g) Flat dilatometer test (DMT) h) In-situ permeability test i. Constant head ii. Falling head iii. Percolation iv. Single packer v. Double packer i) Modulus of subgrade reaction j) Field California bearing ratio test (CBR) k) Optical and acoustic tele-viewer methods 1.3 Sampling a) Disturbed sample (DS) b) Undisturbed Sample (UDS) c) Core recovery d) RQD e) Ground water table 1.4 Testing of soil & rocks A. Soil: 1. Sieve analysis and hydrometer analysis 2. Atterberg’s limits (liquid limit, plastic limit and shrinkage limit) 3. Specific gravity 4. Free swell index 5. Laboratory permeability test (constant head/falling head) 6. Bulk density and moisture content 7. Unconfined compression test (on remoulded or undisturbed sample) 8. Swelling pressure (on undisturbed and remoulded sample) 9. Direct shear test (drained/undrained) (on remoulded or undisturbed sample) 10. Triaxial compression tests (on undisturbed or remoulded sample), i) Unconsolidated undrained, ii) Consolidated undrained test with/without the measurement of pore water pressure, and iii) Consolidated drained 11. Consolidation test; and 12. Vane shear test B. Rock: 1. Visual classification; 2. Water absorption; 3. Porosity; 4. Density; 5. Specific gravity; 6. Hardness; 7. Slake durability; 8. Uniaxial compression test (both saturated and unsaturated); 9. Point load strength index; 10. Deformability test (both saturated and unsaturated); 11. Tensile strength (Brazilian); and 12. Triaxial compression test. 1.5 Foundation suggestion • Shallow Foundation: Bearing capacity and Settlement • Deep Foundation: Load carrying capacity of pile and Bearing capacity of well 2.0 Pavement Investigation: 2.1 Field Testing: 1. Dynamic cone penetration test (DCPT) 2. Benkelman Beam Deflection Test 3. Axle load test 4. Field CBR test 5. Modulus of Sub-grade reaction test 6. Ev2 test 2.2 Laboratory Testing: 1. Sieve analysis and hydrometer analysis 2. Atterberg’s limits (liquid limit, plastic limit and shrinkage limit) 3. Specific gravity 4. Free swell index 5. Bulk density and moisture content 6. California Bearing Ratio test 7. Proctor Compaction test 8. Consolidation test 3.0 Ground Improvement: Various techniques of ground improvement including soil reinforcement and soil nailing 4.0 Slope Stability Stability analysis of slopes/embankment/cutting/dam/land slides
