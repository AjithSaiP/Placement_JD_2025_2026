Job Description

Location: Mumbai/Delhi About the role Your role will encompass all aspects of fire safety engineering. This will range from preparing fire strategies in co-ordination with the engineering design team to the inspection and assessment of fire safety management and protection measures in existing buildings/facilities. As part of this, you will have the opportunity to liaise with external designers and other construction and building professionals on a wide range of projects; experience and develop your ability to use fire-specific design software; conduct research in a variety of skills groups; and have the opportunity to visit sites to monitor the implementation of fire strategies and learn about the installation and construction of fire safety systems. From day one, you’ll be given responsibility to help contribute to a range of varied and high- profile clients but will also be supported in learning about fire engineering and developing as a fire engineer. We encourage all employees to work towards a professional qualification and you will be given support in this endeavour as well. What we look for We’re looking for graduates with integrity, enthusiasm and passion to learn about fire engineering. If that is you, we’d like to hear from you. We’re looking for candidates with the following qualifications, attributes and skills: • Excellent written English, and able to provide evidence/example of own work, if requested • Strong numerical skills • Proficient in the use of Microsoft Word and Excel Desirable skills: • Experience with Autodesk software such as Revit, Navisworks or AutoCAD • Experience reviewing drawings of buildings in 2D and 3D • Experience using sketching tools such as SketchUp • Experience with VBA or other programming languages Behaviours • Flexible to the needs of the business, and changes to project plans • Demonstrate an organised approach to work and meeting deadlines • Demonstrate a creative approach to problem solving • Able to work effectively both individually and as part of a team • Able to use your own initiative, be inquisitive and proactive • Dedicated to the delivery of projects, and the development of the team and business as a whole We would be interested to hear of any community engagement/charity/voluntary work undertaken.
