Job Description

About the Role JFSL offers a unique opportunity for growth and learning by gaining hands-on experience in the largest start-up in the country. You will receive training and mentorship from our experienced leaders across the organisation. You will also be a part of various projects making a tangible impact and have an opportunity to work in cross functional teams. The roles in JFSL will allow you to innovate and partner in creating value that will reshape the industry. Key Responsibilities ● Data Collection & Analysis: ○ Assist in gathering and cleaning large datasets from various sources ○ Analyze historical and real-time data to identify trends, patterns, and insights ○ Develop reports and dashboards for stakeholders based on business requirements ● Machine Learning : ○ Collaborate with senior team members to implement and validate AI/ML models for customer segmentation, recommendation, next best offers etc. ○ Conduct exploratory data analysis (EDA) to uncover potential machine learning opportunities ● AI & Generative AI (LLM): ○ Assist in building and testing AI-enabled solutions such as chatbots, recommendation engines, and decision support tools ○ Help design and refine prompts and instructions for large language models (LLMs) to improve response quality and relevance ○ Support implementation of AI workflows (e.g., retrieval-augmented generation, tool-calling/agentic flows) ○ Contribute to evaluation of AI models by comparing responses, tagging errors, and documenting edge cases and improvement ● Data Visualization: ○ Create interactive and insightful visualizations using tools like Tableau or Power BI ○ Present data findings in a clear and actionable format to business users ● Business Collaboration: ○ Work closely with cross-functional teams (marketing, finance, operations) to understand business objectives and provide data-driven solutions ○ Communicate complex analytical results to non-technical stakeholders effectively ● Learning and Development: ○ Stay updated with the latest trends in data science, machine learning, and AI to bring innovative solutions to the team ○ Participate in internal training and knowledge-sharing sessions Skills required ● Technical Skills: Must-have- ○ Basic proficiency in Python and SQL for data analysis ○ Experience with CSV/Excel files and familiarity with JSON / Rest APIs ○ Understanding of descriptive statistics (mean, median, variance), correlations, hypothesis testing at a basic level ○ Exposure to at least one BI / visualization tool (e.g., Tableau, Power BI, or similar) ○ Conceptual understanding of regression, classification, train/validation/test splits, and model evaluation metrics ○ Basic familiarity with Git / Azure devops (clone, commit, push, pull, branching) Good to have- ○ Hands-on exposure to libraries such as scikit-learn; awareness of TensorFlow/PyTorch is a plus ○ Experience using LLMs (e.g., ChatGPT, Gemini, etc.) for analytical or automation tasks ○ Basic understanding of prompts, few-shot examples, and using LLMs via APIs or tools ○ Exposure to any cloud platform (GCP / AWS / Azure) ○ Familiarity with data warehouses or big data tools like Spark/Big query ● Soft Skills: ○ Strong analytical and problem-solving skills ○ Excellent communication skills to present findings to non-technical stakeholders ○ Ability to work collaboratively in a team environment ○ Detail-oriented with the ability to manage multiple tasks
