Job Description

Absolute is a bioscience company committed to a future where both people and the planet succeed. We combine nature, science, and forward‑thinking innovation to build solutions shaping Agriculture, Human Health, and Sustainable Materials. With a presence in more than 30 countries, our Agtech work includes climate‑focused tools, soil testing, crop insurance, and tech‑enabled trading. Our research is led by Xenesis, a team of over 150 scientists making major discoveries. Absolute is supported by investors such as Sequoia, Tiger Global, Alpha Wave, Nadir Godrej, Kamal Aggarwal, and Kunal Shah. Job Description We are hiring a Research Associate/Sr. Research Associate with expertise in synthetic biology and microbial genetics. Key Responsibilities: Work with laboratory strains of Escherichia coli, fungi, yeast etc using aseptic technique for culturing, maintenance, and transformation. Perform advanced molecular biology techniques like recombinant cloning, assembling, genome editing and recombinant knockouts. Perform genome editing of micro-organisms for improved phenotype and heterologous production of recombinant molecules. Carry out fermentation studies for recombinant strains for yields and scale up studies. Design, implement, and characterize novel gene constructs. Metabolic engineering of recombinant strains and analyzing flux. Prepare and execute established methods and SOPs. Deliver high-quality results in a timely manner and communicate the same with stakeholders. Drive process improvements Maintain all equipment, data and records as per specifications.
