Job Description

The Role Neuron is seeking an AI Infrastructure Engineer to design, build, and operate the foundational infrastructure that enables our agentic strategy. This is a hands on senior individual contributor role responsible for building the core systems that allow engineers across Neuron to develop, evaluate, deploy, and operate multi agent AI systems safely, cost effectively, and at scale without reliance on external vendors. The role is infra first and systems led, treating LLMs as distributed infrastructure components rather than experimental tools. You will work closely with senior engineering leadership, AI Tech Leads, and platform engineers to establish robust, production grade agentic foundations while actively supporting adoption across the wider engineering organisation. Key responsibilities of this role include: • Design and build the core agent runtime, supporting multi agent orchestration, long running and ephemeral agents, and controlled tool usage. • Own the model abstraction and routing layer, enabling multiple LLM providers with evaluation driven promotion, graceful degradation, and cost aware execution. • Implement and operate evaluation pipelines to assess agent outputs, tool usage quality, failure modes, and overall system effectiveness. • Establish comprehensive observability across agent behaviour, model usage, latency, reliability, and cost. • Design and manage long term agent memory and knowledge systems, including vector stores, indexing pipelines, and organisational runbooks. • Define and enforce security guardrails, safety mechanisms, permission boundaries, rate limits, and kill switches. • Ensure all systems meet enterprise grade governance, auditability, and regulatory requirements. • Enable AI first developer workflows through platform primitives, reference implementations, documentation, and mentoring. • Write production grade code regularly, contributing directly to the evolution of the platform. The Requirements Critical • Strong background in distributed systems, AI platform engineering, or backend infrastructure. • Ability to design systems under cost, security, reliability, and regulatory constraints. • Strong knowledge of multi agent architectures, orchestration patterns, and tool using agents. • Experience implementing model routing, abstraction layers, and evaluation driven promotion strategies. • Deep understanding of observability, evaluation, and cost control for AI systems. • Strong experience with cloud infrastructure, with an Azure first mindset and exposure to AWS. • Excellent communication and collaboration skills. • Strong builder mindset with the ability to operate autonomously in ambiguous environments. Preferred • Experience building and operating production LLM or agentic infrastructure within a corporate environment. • Familiarity with cloud-based architectures (min. Azure & AWS) and containerised environments (Kubernetes). • Experience designing AI governance, safety, and guardrail mechanisms in regulated environments. • Familiarity with vector databases, retrieval systems, and long term memory architectures. • Experience enabling AI native SDLC practices. • Experience mentoring engineers and setting technical standards. Role-Specific Tools & Technologies • Core Tools: MuleSoft, REST APIs, MongoDB. • Cloud & Infrastructure: AWS, Azure, Terraform, Kubernetes Success In This Role Looks Like • Neuron operates a robust, secure, and cost effective internal agentic platform with minimal third party reliance. • Engineering teams can safely build, evaluate, and deploy agentic workflows using internal platforms and standards. • Multiple LLMs are leveraged interchangeably through a stable routing and evaluation layer. • Agent behaviour, cost, and performance are observable, measurable, and optimised. • Security, governance, and regulatory requirements are embedded by design. • The role is recognised as a core technical authority enabling Neuron’s agentic transformation.
